## How to read this
- **Base fabric assumed:** 100% cotton or cotton/poly for Everyday and Glitter. **Polyester or nylon** for SportFlex (it won't stick to high-cotton blends). A **light-colored, high-polyester blank** for Infusible Ink (Cricut blanks are the tested case).
- **Temp** = plate setting. **Time** = main press. **Reverse** = flip the item and press the back (Cricut EasyPress method).
- **Pressure:** Cricut defines *gentle* as one hand, about 5–10 lb of body weight, and *firm* as two hands, about 15–20 lb [H-EPFAQ].
- **Peel:** *Cool* = let the liner cool fully before peeling. *Warm* = peel while it's warm, not hot. Peeling at the wrong temperature is a top cause of lifting [H-EPRS].
- Cells marked **CONFIRM** are ones where our sources disagree or don't say. The bot must tell the user to check the official Heat Guide for that cell.
- **Every press:** firm, flat, waist-high surface (no ironing board). Prewash with no fabric softener. Preheat the blank (about 5 s with EasyPress, 10–15 s with an iron) to drive out moisture. Wait 24 h before the first wash, then wash and dry inside out with no bleach [H-QRC] [H-EVERYDAY] [C-WASHFAQ].

## Coach rules tied to this matrix
1. **Brand first.** Ask "Which iron-on brand and type, and which press?" Settings differ by brand (Cricut vs Siser), so use the maker's own spec.
2. **One hidden test spot first** (inside hem or a scrap of the same fabric) [H-HEATRS].
3. **No-stick fix:** re-cover with the liner or a pressing sheet and press another **10–15 s** [H-IRONTS] [H-HEATTS].
4. **Layering:** don't put anything on top of glitter, holographic, foil or flocked iron-on. They can go on top of Everyday. SportFlex layers only with SportFlex. Iron-on can go **on top of** a finished Infusible Ink transfer, never under it [GLITTER-PDF] [H-SPECIALTY] [H-IIFAQ].
5. **Safety:** heat plates burn. Keep kids clear, never leave an iron face-down or unattended, and use the safety base.
6. **Escalate** if an EasyPress won't heat, shows an error, or won't hold temperature. Point to Cricut's EasyPress troubleshooting / Member Care, with no DIY repair [H-EPTS].
